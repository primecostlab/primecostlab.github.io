Questions

Will it work in Google Sheets?

Yes. The file uses only standard functions, no macros and no add-ins. Upload it to Google Drive and open it with Sheets; formatting and formulas carry over. Excel 2016 or later on Windows and Mac works as well.

What does the free calculator do that the Toolkit does not — and the reverse?

The free calculator covers one month of actuals: food, beverage, labor and prime cost from 7 numbers, color-coded. The Toolkit adds a 12-month P&L, menu pricing for 20 items, break-even per day / per seat / per service, a 3-year projection with loan schedule and DSCR, three presets and 76 documented assumptions. If you only need to check last month, the free file is enough.

Where do the benchmarks come from?

Each one is listed on the Assumptions & Sources sheet with publisher, title, year and link: MarginEdge (2026), Backbar Academy (2019), National Restaurant Association (2025), Restaurant365, Black Box Intelligence (2025), Zest Specialty Coffee Roasters (2025) among others. Where no reliable source exists, the sheet says 'No benchmark' rather than inventing one.

Can I use it for a bar, a cafe or a food truck?

Yes. Three presets ship in the file (80-seat restaurant, 60-seat bar, 30-seat cafe / food truck) with segment-specific targets. Replace the light-blue inputs block by block with your own numbers.

Is it bank-ready?

The 3-Year Projection prints on one page with the figures a lender asks for: annual P&L, loan schedule, DSCR, sources and uses. It is a planning model built by a finance analyst with SMB-financing experience, not a substitute for your accountant's statements.
