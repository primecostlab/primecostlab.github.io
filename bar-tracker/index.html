What it does, in one evening

  • Inventory — 50 products (liquor, beer, wine), beginning + purchases − ending = usage, usage cost by category, cost per ounce for recipes, and a variance counter that flags impossible counts.
  • Pour Cost — liquor, beer, wine and total pour cost %, each against a sourced range (BevSpot 2016 by category; MarginEdge 2026 for total beverage cost), green / yellow / red.
  • Cocktail Recipes — 20 cocktails, 5 ingredients each pulled from your inventory: cost per serving, suggested menu price at an 18 / 20 / 22% target (rounded up to the next $0.25), gap with your current price, contribution margin per month.
  • Dashboard Bar — the five numbers a manager checks weekly, the top 5 cocktails by total margin, and a pour cost vs benchmark chart.
  • Monthly P&L Bar — 12 months with labor, occupancy, operating expenses and G&A: prime cost and EBITDA with their ratios.
  • Assumptions & Sources — publisher, title, year and link for every benchmark; where none is reliable, the sheet says "No benchmark".
