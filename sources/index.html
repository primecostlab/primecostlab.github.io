This is the same table that ships on the Assumptions & Sources sheet of the Restaurant Financial Toolkit: 24 ranges with a named source, and 12 places where we found no reliable public source and therefore show no benchmark at all. If you know a better source for one of them, email outilfinance@gmail.com.

How to read it: the range drives the green / yellow / red color on the Dashboard. A range marked "(not sourced)" on one side means only the other end has a source. Some publishers are software vendors or industry blogs, not associations; the note says so when it matters.
