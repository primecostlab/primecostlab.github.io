Why the numbers can be trusted

  • Every benchmark is sourced: food cost 28–35% (MarginEdge, 2026), beverage cost 18–24% (Backbar Academy, 2019), labor 34.2–36.5% (National Restaurant Association, 2025), prime cost 60–65% (Restaurant365), hourly turnover ~92% full service and ~110% limited service (Black Box Intelligence, 2025). Where no reliable source exists, the sheet says "No benchmark".
  • Every calculated cell is a formula. Automated tests check for zero errors on all presets and that the control results match within 0.5%.
  • Excel 2016+ and Google Sheets. No macros, no subscription.

What it is not

A planning model, not accounting software or tax advice. It does not connect to your POS. Seat turns per service are shown as reference only, and the food truck preset shares the cafe benchmarks.
